PAY AFTER THE DEALER CONFIRMS

FLOW
1. Customer places the order at checkout with NO payment and no payment method (checkout step 04 explains this).
2. Dealer confirms the order (dealer orders page). The customer is sent an SMS (Africa's Talking) and an email (SMTP)
   with a link to their order page. Sent once per order. Needs AT_* and SMTP_* in .env, and APP_BASE_URL for the link.
3. On the order page the customer chooses Cash on delivery, M-Pesa or Bank.
   - Cash: tracker step shows "Payment on delivery"; the dealer confirms the cash (as before).
   - M-Pesa / Bank: customer pays to the GasHub account and sends the code; admin confirms (as before).
   - Once paid the tracker step shows "Paid".
   GASHUB COINS are part of this payment step (not checkout): on the order page, above the payment methods, the customer
   can turn coins into a discount (toggle + slider + Apply). The total drops, then they pay by M-Pesa or Bank.
   - Coins are online-only. Applying coins hides Cash; if the customer had picked cash they are asked to choose again.
   - Coins can be changed or removed until a payment code is waiting for GasHub to confirm it.
   - POST /api/my/orders/:id/points { points }  (0 removes). Coins leave the balance when applied; they come back if
     removed or if the order is cancelled. Earned points are still given when the order is paid.
   THE CHECKOUT OPENS UNDER THE ORDER: when the dealer confirms, the order page shows a highlighted "Checkout" card right under
   the order summary (order breakdown, GasHub Coins, Cash / M-Pesa / Bank, payment details) and scrolls to it once.
   AFTER THE CUSTOMER PAYS THE ORDER CONTINUES: the card turns into "Payment received", and only then can the order go out:
   - online (M-Pesa / Bank): the dealer cannot send it out and the messenger cannot start the delivery until GasHub has
     confirmed the money (payment_status = paid). Cash on delivery: allowed at once (the messenger collects the cash).
   - the rule is paymentClearForDelivery() in backend/utils/payment-flow.js, used by dealer-order-controller and tracking-controller.
4. Server rules: M-Pesa prompt and payment-code endpoints refuse until the order is confirmed and a method is chosen.
   Orders that used GasHub points cannot choose cash.

CHECKOUT PAGE (customer-checkout.html) AFTER CONFIRMATION - latest change
- The confirmation SMS and email link to customer-checkout.html?orderId=<order number>&pay=1 (backend/utils/payment-flow.js).
- customer-checkout.html has a PAY MODE for that link: the normal "place your order" form is hidden and only the payment shows
  (order total + GasHub Coins, Cash / M-Pesa / Bank, M-Pesa prompt or code). Script: frontend/customer-checkout-pay.js.
  Without orderId + pay=1 the page works exactly as before (the small gate script in the html loads customer-checkout.js).
- When the payment is done (paid, code sent, or cash chosen) a full-screen success message shows ~3 seconds, then
  customer-orders.html?orderId=...&view=order opens and the rest of the steps (tracker, messenger, delivery, points) continue.
- Opening the app (customer-home.html) or an unpaid confirmed order on customer-orders.html sends the customer to this
  checkout (customer-pay-prompt.js, once per order per session). ?view=order on the orders page skips that redirect.

M-PESA / BANK ROUTES + CODE CHECKING: see ENV-PAYMENT-FIELDS.txt (env block to paste, one-time steps, route list).

MINIMUM COINS TO SPEND (admin) + COINS ON THE CHECKOUT PAGE
- Admin > Settings > "GasHub Coins & Exchange Rate": new field "Coins a customer must have before they can spend them" (saved with the rate;
  stored in the "points" setting as minRedeem, default 30). The server uses it everywhere (checkout coins, profile, /api/points/config).
- customer-checkout.html pay mode: coins are offered only AFTER the customer picks M-Pesa or Bank (not cash). Switching "Activate my coins" on
