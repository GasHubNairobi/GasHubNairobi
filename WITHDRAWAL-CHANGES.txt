WITHDRAWAL CHANGES
1. Customer gets an EMAIL and an SMS with the withdrawal details as soon as a request is sent (and on approve / paid / reject).
   Email needs SMTP_* in .env (already set). SMS needs AT_USERNAME and AT_API_KEY in .env (see INSTALL-MESSAGING.txt);
   without them the SMS is skipped and the server log says so.
2. The conversion rate is no longer shown to customers (page, API, email, SMS). Admin still sees it.
3. Admin > Withdrawals: "Open / Close Withdrawals" switch + optional "Next withdrawals open on" date and time (Nairobi time).
4. Customers see a countdown "Withdrawals will be available in ..." while closed; the server also blocks requests.
   When the time ends withdrawals open by themselves. No database migration needed.

UPDATE 2
5. Admin: ON/OFF button (saves instantly, no checkbox). While OFF you can set the time they open again.
6. Admin: "Payout Account" card = the account the money is sent from (M-Pesa phone / Paybill / Till / Bank). It is shown with the
   exact amount in the Approve and Mark-paid dialogs.
7. Amount follows the CURRENT conversion rate: waiting/approved requests are worth points x today's rate (admin and customer see it),
   and Approve / Mark paid settle at today's rate, even if the request was made before the rate changed.
8. If the browser shows 404 for /api/admin/withdrawals/settings: stop and fully restart the Node server so it loads the updated project.

UPDATE 3
9.  Customer page while withdrawals are closed: only the countdown (when a reopening time is set), or "Withdrawals are unavailable at
    the moment" with a "Notify me when they are back" button. No balance, form or history is shown.
10. Customers who tap Notify get an email + SMS when withdrawals open (admin switches ON, or the scheduled time arrives; checked every minute).
11. Admin card renamed "GasHub Withdrawal Account". It can also come from the .env lines in ENV-WITHDRAWAL-ACCOUNT.txt.
12. Customer pages, emails and SMS now say "GasHub Coins" and show the official GasHub coin.

UPDATE 4
13. Withdrawal charges: Admin > Withdrawals > "Withdrawal Charges". Ranges in GasHub Coins or KSh, first range starts at the lowest
    withdrawal amount, "+ Add range" for more, last range is "and above". Each range has a transaction charge (KSh).
    Amount sent to the customer = amount withdrawn - charge. Charges are shown to customers (list + live "You will receive").
    Waiting/approved requests use today's rate AND today's charges when approved / paid.
14. Records: every withdrawal stays as a record (admin list up to 5000, "Download records" CSV; customer sees "My withdrawal records",
    also while withdrawals are closed). Records are never deleted by these pages.
15. New customer route POST /api/customer/withdrawals/quote and POST /api/customer/withdrawals/notify (customer-account-routes.js).
    "API endpoint not found" on notify / settings means the running server has not loaded the new files: replace the files and fully
    restart Node. The database gets two new columns (fee_ksh, net_ksh) by itself on first use.

UPDATE 5
16. Withdrawal Charges: From, To and Charge are all typeable in every range (the last range's To can stay empty = "and above").
    Ranges must not overlap. A clear "GasHub Coins | KSh" switch chooses what the ranges measure.
17. Withdrawal Limits: new "Maximum GasHub Coins per withdrawal" box next to the lowest amount (empty = no limit). Enforced by the server;
    customers see "Most per withdrawal: N GasHub Coins".
18. When a withdrawal is marked Paid the customer gets an email and an SMS: "KSh X has been deposited to your M-Pesa ... Keep shopping with GasHub".

UPDATE 6
19. Admin > Withdrawals > "Schedule Payouts": choose the date and time the money is sent to customers. Every waiting / approved request
    is then sent automatically to M-Pesa and marked Paid when M-Pesa confirms. Customers see the payout date on their withdraw page.
20. "Send now" button next to each waiting / approved request: sends that customer's money instantly (Safaricom B2C). The row shows
    "Sending to M-Pesa..." and turns Paid by itself. Failed payments show the reason and "Send again".
21. Needs the MPESA_B2C_* lines in .env, see ENV-AUTO-PAYOUT.txt. Fully restart Node after replacing the files.
22. Customer withdraw page no longer flashes hidden parts on refresh (the hidden attribute now always wins).
