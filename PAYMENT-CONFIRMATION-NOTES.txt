PAYMENT CONFIRMATION + ORDER COMPLETION

RULES
- CASH: only the dealer can confirm it (button "Confirm cash received" on the dealer orders page).
- M-PESA / BANK: the customer pays into the GasHub account the admin set. Dealers CANNOT confirm these (the server refuses).
  The customer sends the M-Pesa code on their order page; it shows as "Confirming your payment". GasHub then checks its M-Pesa
  statement and confirms it in Admin > Payments (open the pending payment > Confirm money received, or Reject code).
  Money with no code sent: Admin > Payments > "Confirm M-Pesa Money Received" (order number + code).
- PAID: set when the money is confirmed. The customer gets their GasHub points then.
- COMPLETED: a delivery order completes by itself when it is both delivered (messenger confirmed) and paid, whichever happens last.
  A pickup order is completed by the dealer once it is paid and collected.
- The dealer can no longer mark a delivery order delivered/completed by hand, and nothing can be completed while unpaid.
- MESSENGER: the confirm button appears only when about 1 minute from the customer (distance x 1.3 road factor at the phone's
  speed, 25 km/h when parked: about 320 m at walking/parked speed). The server re-checks the phone's last position when the
  button is pressed. Orders with no delivery pin cannot be measured, so the button shows once the delivery has started.
  The messenger page also tells them whether to collect cash or not.

SETUP (once)
1) Supabase SQL Editor: run backend/payment-confirmation-migration.sql (safe to run again).
2) Restart the server.
3) Admin > Settings > "GasHub Payment Account (M-Pesa)": enter your Paybill / Till / phone and Save.
   Until it is saved, customers are told to contact support to pay.
