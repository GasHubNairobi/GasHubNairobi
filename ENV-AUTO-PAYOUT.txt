AUTOMATIC PAYOUT TO CUSTOMERS (Admin > Withdrawals > "Schedule Payouts" and the "Send now" button)

The money is sent straight to the customer's M-Pesa with Safaricom Daraja B2C. Until these lines are in .env the admin page
shows "Automatic payout not set up" and Send now / scheduling say so (nothing is ever marked Paid without M-Pesa confirming).
Paying by hand and pressing "Mark paid" still works exactly as before.

1. In the Safaricom Daraja portal, get your app approved for B2C (Go Live). You will get:
     - the B2C shortcode (the Paybill the money is sent FROM)
     - an Initiator name and Initiator password
2. Turn the Initiator password into a SecurityCredential: encrypt it with Safaricom's public certificate
   (SandboxCertificate.cer for testing, ProductionCertificate.cer for live) and base64 it. Daraja's portal has a tool for this.
3. Add these lines to .env (the existing MPESA_CONSUMER_KEY / MPESA_CONSUMER_SECRET / MPESA_CALLBACK_BASE_URL /
   MPESA_CALLBACK_SECRET / MPESA_ENV lines from ENV-PAYMENT-FIELDS.txt are reused):

     MPESA_B2C_INITIATOR_NAME=your_initiator_name
     MPESA_B2C_SECURITY_CREDENTIAL=the_long_encrypted_base64_string
     # optional: only if the B2C shortcode is different from MPESA_SHORTCODE
     MPESA_B2C_SHORTCODE=
     # optional: BusinessPayment (default) or SalaryPayment
     MPESA_B2C_COMMAND=BusinessPayment

4. In the Daraja portal set the B2C Result URL and Queue Timeout URL for your shortcode to:
     <MPESA_CALLBACK_BASE_URL>/api/payments/mpesa/b2c/result/<MPESA_CALLBACK_SECRET>
     <MPESA_CALLBACK_BASE_URL>/api/payments/mpesa/b2c/timeout/<MPESA_CALLBACK_SECRET>
   (the app also sends them with every payment). The address must be public HTTPS.
5. Fully restart the Node server.

HOW IT WORKS
- Send now (next to every waiting / approved request): sends that customer's money immediately, at today's rate and charge, rounded to whole
  shillings. The row shows "Sending to M-Pesa..." and turns Paid by itself when Safaricom confirms; the customer gets the usual
  "deposited" email + SMS. Mark paid and Reject are blocked while a payment is on its way, so it can never be paid twice.
- If M-Pesa refuses or fails, the row shows the reason and the button becomes "Send again". The customer's coins stay held.
  If there was no answer at all, check your M-Pesa statement before pressing Send again.
- Schedule Payouts: pick a date and time (Nairobi). At that time every waiting / approved request that has not been tried yet is sent,
  one after the other. Customers see "GasHub will send withdrawals to M-Pesa on ..." on their withdraw page. The result of the last run
  is shown under the schedule. Clear schedule removes it.
- The database gets new columns on first use (payout_state, payout_receipt, ...). No migration to run.

AUTOMATIC REFUNDS (Admin > Payments > "Refunds to send")
- The same B2C lines above also power refunds. With them set, press "Refund now" next to a refund and the money goes straight back to the
  M-Pesa number the customer paid with. The order turns "refunded" only when Safaricom confirms, the refunded amount is taken off the
  order (amount paid) and the payment is marked refunded, so the totals and reports no longer count it. The customer gets an SMS + email.
- M-Pesa sends whole shillings: a refund of KSh 1,500.40 is sent as KSh 1,500.
- Orders paid by bank transfer cannot be refunded to M-Pesa automatically: send it through the bank and press "Mark as refunded".
- If M-Pesa fails, the row shows the reason and the button becomes "Refund again". If there was no answer at all, check your M-Pesa
  statement before pressing it again.
