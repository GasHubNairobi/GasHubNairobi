Bank Account Details

These are the details customers see when they choose Bank. Saving here updates the customer screens straight away.

What customers will seeNot set yetUntil this is saved, customers who choose Bank are told the bank details are being set up.

How you confirm a bank payment: the customer sends the bank reference from their receipt. It shows in Payments as a pending payment. Check that the money (the full amount) is in your bank account, then open it and press Confirm money received. The order then shows as paid and the customer gets their points. If you cannot find the money, press Reject and the customer is asked to check the reference.

Backup M-Pesa Till (manual payment)

Customers never see this at first. It appears on their payment screen only if the M-Pesa phone prompt failed to reach them twice (or when the phone prompt is switched off above). They pay this Till, then enter the M-Pesa code. Money here goes to GasHub, not the dealer, and you confirm it in the Payments page.
