1. How delivery works

  1. You choose a product and place your order, choosing delivery or pickup.
  2. The dealer confirms the order.
  3. You pay (online or cash, as allowed). Online payments must be confirmed before the gas is sent out.
  4. The dealer assigns a messenger who delivers to your pinned location. You can follow progress on your order page, and track the delivery when live tracking is available.
  5. You check the gas, pay cash if it is a cash order, and the order is completed.

2. Delivery areas and charges

  • Each dealer delivers within the area they choose. If a dealer cannot deliver to you, they may reject the order or you may choose pickup.
  • The delivery fee (if any) is shown before you place your order. Pickup is free.
  • You will not be charged any delivery fee that was not shown to you before ordering.

3. Delivery times

Delivery times shown in the app are estimates, based on the distance between the dealer and you. Real time depends on the dealer, traffic, weather and whether you can be reached. Dealers only deliver during their working hours. Orders placed when a dealer is closed will wait for them to open, or you may choose another dealer.

4. Your address and phone

  • Pin your location on the map and add clear directions or a landmark.
  • Keep your phone on, with network, and be ready to answer. The messenger will call when close.
  • Make sure you or someone you trust is there to receive the gas.

5. When your gas arrives

  • The messenger should be able to show who they are. Check that the order matches what you ordered.
  • Check the cylinder: the seal, valve and any regulator or hose. Do not accept a damaged or leaking cylinder.
  • For cash orders, pay only after you have checked the gas.
  • Tell us right away if something is wrong. Do not use gas you think is unsafe.

6. Missed or failed delivery

If the messenger cannot find you, cannot reach you, or nobody can receive the gas, the dealer may cancel the delivery or try again. A dealer may charge a fair re-delivery fee if the failure was caused by wrong details, but they must tell you first. If the order was paid online and is cancelled, our Refund and Cancellation Policy applies.

7. Calling the dealer and messenger

You can call the dealer and the messenger while your order is open, and for 2 days after it ends. After that, their numbers are hidden to protect their privacy. For later problems, use the complaint option or contact us.

8. Gas safety at home

  • Keep cylinders upright, in a ventilated place, away from heat and flames.
  • Never use a cylinder or hose that looks damaged. Check for leaks with soapy water, not a flame.
  • If you smell gas: close the valve, open windows, avoid switches and flames, move outside and call the dealer or emergency services.

9. If something goes wrong

Late, wrong, damaged or missing orders can be reported through the complaint option on the order or by contacting us. See the Refund and Cancellation Policy for what can be done.
