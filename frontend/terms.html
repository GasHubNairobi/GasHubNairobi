1. About GasHub and these terms

GasHub is an online marketplace in Kenya that connects customers with gas (LPG) dealers for the sale and delivery of gas, cylinders and accessories. These Terms of Use are the rules for using the GasHub website and app. By creating an account, signing in or placing an order, you agree to them.

If you do not agree, please do not use GasHub. These terms should be read together with our Privacy Policy, Refund and Cancellation Policy and Delivery Policy.

2. Your account

  • You must be 18 years or older to use GasHub.
  • Give true information (your name, phone number, email and delivery details) and keep it up to date.
  • Keep your password private. You are responsible for what happens on your account, so tell us straight away if you think someone else is using it.
  • One person should keep one customer account. Accounts must not be shared, sold or transferred.

3. What GasHub does, and what dealers do

GasHub provides the platform where customers find dealers, compare prices, order, pay and track their orders. Dealers are independent businesses. The dealer sets the product prices, confirms or rejects your order, prepares the gas and arranges delivery through their own messengers.

For online payments (M-Pesa and bank), the money is paid to GasHub first and is later paid out to the dealer after GasHub’s commission, as explained in the dealer section below.

4. Placing an order

  • When you place an order it is sent to the dealer. It is not final until the dealer confirms it. A dealer can reject an order, for example when they are closed, out of stock or cannot reach your location.
  • You pay after the dealer confirms. You will be told by notification, SMS or email when you can pay.
  • Prices are set by the dealer and shown before you place the order. The delivery fee, if any, is also shown before you place the order.
  • If a dealer is outside their working hours, the app shows them as closed.
  • Check your delivery address, pinned location and phone number before you place an order. Wrong details can delay or cancel your delivery.

5. Paying safely

  • You can pay by M-Pesa (a prompt on your phone), by bank when it is switched on, or with cash on delivery where the dealer allows it.
  • Enter your M-Pesa PIN only on your own phone. GasHub will never ask you for your PIN, your password or a code sent to you.
  • Only pay through the payment options shown inside GasHub. Do not send money to a personal number because someone asked you to. If you pay by the backup Till shown in the app, enter the M-Pesa code so we can match the payment to your order.
  • If you were charged but your order does not show as paid, contact us with the M-Pesa code. We will check it against our records and fix it or refund it.

6. GasHub points

  • You may earn GasHub points for certain activity, such as paid orders, and use points as a discount on online payments. Points are not money, cannot be transferred, and have no value outside GasHub, except where GasHub lets you withdraw them.
  • The number of points earned, the discount they give, and any withdrawal limits and charges are set by GasHub and shown in the app. We may change them from time to time.
  • If an order is cancelled, points you spent on it are returned and points earned from it are taken back.
  • Points obtained through cheating, fake orders, repeated accounts or other abuse (sometimes called “coin farming”) may be removed and the account may be suspended.

7. Gas safety

Gas is dangerous if handled badly. When your gas arrives, check the cylinder, seal and fittings before you accept and pay. Do not use a cylinder that is damaged or leaking. If you smell gas, open windows, keep flames and switches away, close the valve and move outside, then call the dealer or emergency services.

Dealers are responsible for the quality of their gas, their cylinders and for having the licences and approvals the law requires. GasHub asks dealers to confirm this, but we cannot inspect every cylinder.

9. Terms for dealers and messengers

  • Dealers must give true business details, hold the licences needed to sell gas in Kenya, and keep prices, stock and working hours accurate.
  • Dealers must confirm or reject orders promptly, deliver what was ordered, and treat customers fairly.
  • Commission and payouts. GasHub keeps a commission on each order at the rate set for the dealer. For online orders, GasHub pays the dealer the amount due, minus commission, on the payout schedule shown in the dealer portal, after a short holding period so that problems can be raised. For cash orders, the dealer collects the cash and the commission is carried forward and taken from later payouts.
  • Orders that are cancelled or refunded are not paid out. If a dealer was paid for an order that is later cancelled or refunded, GasHub may take that amount back from future payouts.
  • A verification badge may be offered for a fee. It shows that GasHub has checked certain details. It is not a guarantee of the dealer’s products.
  • Dealers are responsible for their messengers and for giving GasHub accurate messenger details. Customer details may be used only to complete the delivery.
