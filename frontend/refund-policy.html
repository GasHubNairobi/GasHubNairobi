1. Summary

You can cancel an order in the app until it is out for delivery. If you paid online, GasHub sends your money back to your M-Pesa number and returns any points you used. Cash orders are settled with the dealer. Details are below.

2. When you can cancel

  • You can cancel while your order is pending (waiting for the dealer) or confirmed (accepted but not yet sent out).
  • Once the order is out for delivery or delivered, it can no longer be cancelled in the app. Please contact us or the dealer if there is a problem.
  • If you paid cash and the cash was already received, the order cannot be cancelled in the app.
  • A dealer may also reject or cancel an order, for example when they are out of stock or cannot deliver to you. If that happens before you pay, you owe nothing. If you already paid online, you get a full refund.

3. How to cancel

  1. Open My Orders and choose the order.
  2. Tap Cancel order and give a short reason if asked.
  3. You will see a confirmation. If a refund is due, the order shows the refund amount and its progress.

4. Refunds for online payments (M-Pesa and bank)

Online payments are paid to GasHub, so GasHub (not the dealer) returns your money when a paid order is cancelled.

  • You are refunded the amount you actually paid for the cancelled order.
  • The refund is sent to the M-Pesa number shown on your order. Check that number carefully when you cancel.
  • Your order page shows the refund as pending while we process it and as refunded with the M-Pesa receipt when it has been sent. You will also be notified.
  • M-Pesa and bank charges taken by the payment provider are outside GasHub’s control and are not refunded by us, unless the provider returns them.

5. GasHub points on a cancelled order

Points you spent as a discount on the order are put back in your account. Points you earned from that order are taken back, because the sale did not complete. The discount is never paid out as cash.

6. Cash orders

For cash orders the dealer holds your money, so there is no GasHub refund. If you paid cash and are owed money back (for example for a missing item), the dealer must return it. If the dealer does not, tell us through a complaint and we will follow up with the dealer.

7. Problems with a delivered order

Tell us as soon as you can, ideally on the day of delivery, if your order is:

  • not what you ordered, or short or damaged;
  • leaking, with a broken seal or a faulty fitting;
  • very late, or marked delivered when it was not.

Open the order and use the complaint option, or contact us. Photos help. We will look into it with the dealer. Depending on what we find, the fix may be a replacement, a correction, or a full or partial refund. To protect both sides, GasHub holds the dealer’s payout for a short time after delivery so that problems can be raised before the money is released.

8. Paid twice, or paid but the order is not marked paid

If you were charged twice, or you paid but the order does not show as paid, contact us with your order number and the M-Pesa code. We will check it. If it is a genuine double or wrong payment, we will mark the order paid or refund the extra amount to the paying M-Pesa number. Please do not pay a third time while we check.

9. How long a refund takes

We send refunds as soon as they have been processed. If your refund has not reached you, contact us with your order number and we will check its status straight away. Your mobile-money provider may also need a short time to show the money.

10. When a refund may be refused

  • The order was already delivered correctly, or the problem was reported much too late to check.
  • There is evidence of fraud, fake orders or abuse of points or offers.
  • The order was paid in cash and is a matter between you and the dealer.

If we refuse, we will tell you why. This does not affect any right you have under Kenyan law.
