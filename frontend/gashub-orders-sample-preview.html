How GasHub Points work

GasHub Points reward you for qualifying purchases. The amount of points earned and the cash value of each point are determined by the current settings managed by the GasHub admin.

1Make a qualifying purchase. Points are confirmed according to the live rewards settings and order status.
2Complete three purchases. Your progress increases when each qualifying order is completed.
3Redeem eligible points. Once eligible, use the points available in your account; the system applies the admin-managed exchange rate.
