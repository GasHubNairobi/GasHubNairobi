1. Who we are and who this covers

GasHub is an online platform in Kenya that connects customers with gas (LPG) dealers for the sale and delivery of gas, cylinders and accessories. In this policy, “GasHub”, “we”, “us” and “our” mean the operator of the GasHub website and apps. For the information described here, GasHub is the “data controller” under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

This policy applies to everyone who uses GasHub:

  • Customers who browse, order and pay for products.
  • Dealers who apply to sell on GasHub, or who run a dealer account.
  • Delivery messengers (riders and drivers) who deliver a dealer’s orders.
  • GasHub administrators and support staff who manage the platform.
  • Visitors who open the website without an account.

By creating an account, signing in or using GasHub, you confirm that you have read this policy. Where the law requires your consent for something (for example sharing your device location), we ask for it separately and you can say no.

4. Your location

Location is central to how GasHub works, so we want to be especially clear about it.

  • Your pin. You choose where to place your pin on the map, or you can allow GasHub to use your phone’s current location. We use it to show nearby dealers, to work out distance and delivery fees, and so the dealer and messenger can find you.
  • Your control. Your device only shares its location after you give permission. You can move or remove your pin, and you can turn location access off at any time in your phone or browser settings. If you do, you can still pin a place on the map by hand.
  • Dealers’ locations. A dealer’s business pin is shown to customers so they can find the shop and arrange pickup.
  • What we do not do. We do not follow your phone in the background, and we do not use your location for advertising.

Maps on GasHub are drawn using OpenStreetMap-based map tiles. When a map loads, your browser contacts the map provider, which can see your IP address and the area of map you view.

5. Live order tracking

  • For delivery orders, the messenger can share their phone’s GPS position through a private tracking link so you can see the delivery on a map with an estimated arrival time.
  • Tracking works only while your order is open. When the order is completed or cancelled, the map is removed and the server stops accepting and sending the messenger’s position.
  • The messenger’s tracking link is private to that order, expires after 12 hours, and is locked to the first device that opens it, so it cannot be passed around.
  • The messenger chooses to start and stop sharing, and can stop at any time. Sharing is limited to the delivery.
  • For pickup orders, we show you the dealer’s shop location instead. No live tracking is used.

6. Payments, refunds and points

  • M-Pesa. When you pay with M-Pesa, you receive a prompt on your phone and enter your PIN on your phone, not on GasHub. Safaricom tells us whether the payment succeeded and gives us a receipt number and the paying phone number, which we match to your order.
  • Bank payments. If you pay by bank, we ask for the bank reference so we can confirm the payment.
  • Cash. For cash orders we record that the order was paid in cash.
  • Refunds. If a paid order is cancelled and you are due a refund, we return it using the payment details held for that order and keep a record of it.
  • Dealer payouts. We share with each dealer the sales and payout information for their own orders, including how much was sent and the account that received it. Dealers see this for their own business only.
  • GasHub points. Points are recorded against your account and can be used as a discount on online payments, according to the rules set by GasHub.

7. Who we share it with

We share information only when it is needed to provide GasHub, when you ask us to, or when the law requires it.

Dealers and messengers

  • When you place an order, the dealer fulfilling it sees what they need to complete it: your name, delivery address, delivery pin, the items ordered and your delivery phone number.
  • If the dealer assigns a messenger to your delivery, that messenger sees your name, delivery address and pin, and the order contact needed to deliver.
  • You can see the dealer’s and messenger’s phone number while your order is open and for 2 days after it is completed or cancelled. After that the number is masked.
  • Your password, payment PIN and other customers’ details are never shown to dealers or messengers.

8. Social sign-in, invites and rewards

  • Sign in with Google, Facebook or X. If you use one of these, we receive your name, email address and profile photo (X only shares an email if permitted). We use them to create or find your GasHub account. We do not post anything on your behalf. Your use of those services is governed by their own privacy policies.
  • Inviting friends. You can share an invite link through WhatsApp or other apps. We do not read your contacts. We only record that an invited person signed up through your link so we can award your points.
  • Following us. Links to GasHub’s social accounts open those platforms, which then collect information under their own policies.

9. Messages, reviews, complaints and the assistant

  • Chats. Messages between you and a dealer or GasHub support are stored so both sides can continue the conversation and so we can resolve disputes. Phone numbers stay private inside chat.
  • Reviews and complaints. Reviews are visible to others. Complaints are handled by GasHub and may be shared with the dealer concerned so it can be resolved.
  • GasHub assistant. Messages you type to the assistant are used to answer your question. Please do not type passwords, PINs or full card numbers into the assistant.

10. Notifications and reminders

  • We send service messages you need, such as order confirmations, payment prompts and delivery updates, by email, in the app and, where enabled, SMS.
  • We may send reminders, for example if an order was left unfinished or it has been a while since your last gas order, and offers or announcements. Dealers can receive broadcast messages from GasHub on the email and phone number they gave us.
  • You can turn notifications on or off in your profile Settings, and use the unsubscribe option in emails. Essential account and order messages may still be sent.

13. How we protect it

  • Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes. We cannot read them and we will never ask you for your password by email, SMS or WhatsApp.
  • Access is controlled by role. Customers, dealers and administrators each have separate sign-ins, and a dealer can only reach their own products, orders and payouts.
  • Admin pages require an administrator sign-in.
  • Tracking links are private, expire, and are locked to one device.
  • Payment confirmations from M-Pesa are checked before an order is marked as paid.
  • Only the staff who need information to do their job can see it.

No system is completely secure. Please use a strong, unique password, keep it private, and log out on shared phones and computers. If you think your account has been accessed without permission, contact us straight away (see section 20).

14. Your rights and choices

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your information is used, which is what this policy is for.
  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is wrong or out of date.
  • Delete information we no longer need or have no lawful reason to keep.
  • Object to your information being used for certain purposes, including direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent you have given, for example for location or marketing. This will not affect use that happened before you withdrew it.
  • Receive a copy of the information you gave us in a commonly used format, where this applies.

How to use your rights

  • Update your name, phone, photo, pin and settings yourself from your profile.
  • For access, correction, deletion or objection requests, contact us using the details in section 20. We may ask you to confirm who you are before we act, and we will reply within the time the law requires.
  • Some information we may have to keep (for example payment records) even after you ask for deletion. We will tell you if that applies.

If you are not happy with how we handle your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) in Kenya.

15. Deleting your account

  • You can ask to delete your customer account from your profile once it is 7 days old. You confirm by typing DELETE.
  • Your account is then fully deleted 7 days after your request. You can cancel the request during these 7 days by signing in again.
  • When deletion completes, we remove your profile, saved pin, photo, favourites, followed dealers and similar personal details. Your GasHub points are removed with the account.
  • We may keep order and payment records, in a form that is not tied to your profile where possible, for as long as the law requires.

Dealers and messengers who want their information removed should contact us using the details in section 20. Closing a dealer account does not remove records of past orders, which we must keep for accounting and dispute purposes.

16. Where your data is processed

GasHub is built for Kenya, but some of our service providers (for example cloud hosting, email delivery and image storage) run servers outside Kenya. When your information is processed abroad, we use providers that protect it and we take the steps the Data Protection Act requires for transfers outside Kenya.

17. Children

GasHub is meant for adults who are 18 or older. Buying and delivering gas is not something for children. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

18. Dealers and messengers

Dealers and messengers receive customer information only so they can complete an order. By using GasHub they agree to:

  • use a customer’s name, phone number, address and pin only to fulfil that order and handle questions about it;
  • not copy, sell, share or use it for their own marketing, and delete it when it is no longer needed;
  • keep their login details private and not share their account;
  • share live location only through the private tracking link and only while delivering.

Dealers are responsible for the personal information of any messengers they add (such as ID number and vehicle registration) and must have the messenger’s permission to share it with GasHub. If you believe a dealer or messenger has misused your information, contact us and we will investigate and, where needed, suspend the account.

19. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as GasHub grows or the law changes. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make an important change, we will tell you in the app or by email. If you keep using GasHub after a change, it applies to you from then on.
