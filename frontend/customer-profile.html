Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what we need: your name, phone, email, delivery address, pin, orders and activity.
  • Dealers and messengers see your details only to complete your order.
  • Your location is used for nearby dealers and delivery, and live tracking stops when the order is done.
  • We never sell your personal information.
  • You can edit your details, change your pin, turn off notifications and delete your account.

The full policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and your rights under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.
