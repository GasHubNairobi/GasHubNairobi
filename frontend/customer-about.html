About GasHub

Developed by Vynex Web Solutions
Who we are

A better way to access gas

GasHub is a customer-focused digital platform that connects people looking for gas with dealers who provide gas products and cylinders.

Our platform brings customers and dealers together in one convenient marketplace, reducing the difficulty of finding available gas products and reliable suppliers.

Through GasHub, customers can explore available gas products, discover dealers, review product options and place orders without having to move from one dealer to another.

We are building GasHub around a simple principle: accessing an essential household product should be convenient, straightforward and dependable.

Our story

Built to connect customers and dealers

GasHub was introduced on 26 January 2026 with the aim of creating a simpler way for customers to access gas and connect with gas dealers.

The idea came from recognizing the challenges customers can face when looking for gas, including finding nearby dealers, knowing what products are available and getting convenient access to the products they need.

Our platform is designed to serve both sides of the marketplace. Customers get a convenient way to discover and order gas, while dealers get an opportunity to connect with more customers.

As GasHub continues to grow, we remain focused on improving the customer experience and developing stronger connections between customers and dealers.
